Fortresshq

Internal · not a public service

Someone stands the watch.

Fortress HQ is the internal operations platform behind Digitl. It reads the advertising, analytics and search data of accounts we have been granted access to, and turns it into briefings the team acts on.

It is not a public product and there is nothing here to sign up for. If you are looking for the agency, that is digitl.me.

For reviewers

This application requests access to Google Ads, Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Google Tag Manager on behalf of the operator, for accounts the operator already administers. What it reads, what it does with it, and what it will never do are set out in full in the privacy policy.
